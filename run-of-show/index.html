The six things to remember

  • 8:00 is a cue. Follow Edson's hand down to silence, together, then he plays first. Rehearse it three times.
  • Every speech: music down, Edson talks, music back up. It never stops dead.
  • Pieces are 5 to 10 minutes, each with its own key. All on white keys: A minor is home, C major is the lift, G is the one step out, E phrygian is the chaos.
  • One real key change on purpose: 8:27, E phrygian down to A minor. The band leads it.
  • No cut between Act 2 and Act 3. The rise carries straight through. No solos anywhere.
  • The last song is composed, not improvised. Know it cold.
