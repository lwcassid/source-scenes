The six things to remember

  • 8:00: Edson opens, you follow him in. The opener is Lance's song in C minor with the A♭ minor bridge, and the wall is in C minor with it. Pieces run 5 to 10 minutes; if the set comes in under an hour, fine.
  • Fresco sits out the opener and enters for the launch.
  • In space, no drums. Then the energy comes back up and a sharp cut-off ends Act 1.
  • The ceremony belongs to the guitar. Em riff, tons of space, Edson leads seven minutes, drums are accents.
  • Act 3 is to dance. A minor then C minor. Fresco's breakbeat starts on the Am → Gm.
  • Keys on the wall: Act 1 C minor · Act 2 E minor · Act 3 the wall is silent. The sketches and vibe tracks are for the feel only.
