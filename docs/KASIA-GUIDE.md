# Playing with the SOURCE scenes — the easy guide 🌸

Hi Kasia! This is the projection wall for The Cave: every scene is a little
audiovisual instrument you play with two hands. This guide gets you making
changes to YOUR scenes by talking to Claude in plain English. No coding, no
terminal, nothing to install.

**The live wall:** https://source-interaction-library.netlify.app
**Your scenes:** Ferro Bloom (SRC-15) · Weather Station (SRC-10) · Epicycle Court (SRC-01)

---

## One-time setup (about 10 minutes, once, ever)

1. **Make a free GitHub account** at github.com — this is where the project
   lives. Any username is fine.
2. **Tell Lance your username.** He'll send you an invite to the project —
   click **Accept** in the email GitHub sends you.
3. **Connect Claude to GitHub:** go to **claude.ai/code**, sign in with your
   normal Claude account, click to connect GitHub, allow it, and choose the
   **source-scenes** project.

That's it. Forever.

## Making changes (every time — this is the whole job)

1. Go to **claude.ai/code** → pick **source-scenes** → start a session.
2. Say what you want, like you'd say it to a person:
   - *"Show me screenshots of Weather Station as it is right now."*
   - *"Make a new version of Weather Station where the rain is fatter and slower, and the thunder feels further away."*
   - *"Ferro Bloom's colors feel cold — warm them up a little."*
   - *"I don't get what my left hand does in Epicycle Court — make it more obvious."*
3. Claude already knows all the house rules (they're saved inside the
   project): it will make a **new version** of your scene, look at
   screenshots to check its own work, and publish.
4. Wait a minute, refresh the live site — your scene, new version, in the
   dropdown.

## The version thing (why you cannot break anything)

- **Nothing is ever overwritten.** Every round of changes becomes a new
  version — V2, V3, V4 … — in the dropdown at the top of your scene. Every
  old version stays there forever. So experiment freely.
- **Sharing:** add `#scene=SRC-10` to the site address and it opens Weather
  Station directly, always the newest version. There's also a **LINK** button
  on every scene that copies this for you.
- Want to show someone an exact older version? `#scene=SRC-10.2` pins V2.

## Three rules of the road

1. **Work on your own scenes.** (Yours: SRC-15, SRC-10, SRC-01.) Ask in the
   group chat before touching someone else's.
2. **Say it in the chat when you're jamming** ("working on Weather Station
   tonight") so two people aren't publishing at the same moment.
3. **If something looks wrong, nothing is lost.** Just tell Claude:
   *"the last change broke it — go back to how it was."* Old versions make
   this painless.

## "Do I need to install a skill or anything?"

**No.** All the accumulated knowledge — the design rules, the
projected-on-mesh rules, how the music engine works, how to build and
publish — lives *inside the project itself*. Claude reads it automatically
at the start of every session. You just talk.
