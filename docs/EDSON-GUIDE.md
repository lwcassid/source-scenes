# Playing with the SOURCE scenes — Edson's guide

Hi Edson. This is the projection instrument for The Cave: every scene is a
little audiovisual instrument you play with two hands in the air. You have a
set of your own — **THE TEMPLE SET**, five scenes built as a head start from
the brief (space, thin lines, ceremonial, your dots-and-viscosity) — and this
guide gets you changing them by talking to Claude in plain English. No
coding, nothing to install.

## One-time setup (about 10 minutes, once, ever)

1. **Make a free GitHub account** at github.com — that's where the project
   lives. Any username is fine.
2. **Tell Lance your username.** He'll invite you to the project — click
   **Accept** in the email GitHub sends you.
3. **Connect Claude to GitHub:** go to **claude.ai/code**, sign in with your
   Claude account, click to connect GitHub, allow it, and choose the
   **source-scenes** project.

That's it.

## Making changes (every time — this is the whole job)

1. Go to **claude.ai/code** → pick **source-scenes** → start a session.
2. **Say who you are first** ("This is Edson") — every version you make
   gets your name on it in the scene's HISTORY, and this is how it knows.
3. Say what you want, like you'd say it to a person:
   - *"Show me screenshots of Orbits as it is right now."*
   - *"Make a new version of The Commons where the wind is slower and the stars are bluer."*
   - *"Transmission's kit is too busy — just a kick and a sub, nothing else."*
   - *"In Ascension, the band at the top should be gold, not white, and the drone should climb slower."*
   - *"Reorder the Temple set: Orbits before Vespers."*
4. Claude knows the house rules (they're saved inside the project): it will
   make a **new version** of your scene, look at screenshots of its own work,
   and publish.
5. Wait a minute, refresh the live site — your scene, new version, in the
   dropdown.

## The version thing (why you cannot break anything)

- **Nothing is ever overwritten.** Every round becomes a new version — V2,
  V3, V4 … — in the dropdown on your scene. Every old version stays there
  forever. Experiment freely.
- **Sharing:** `#scene=SRC-54` opens Transmission directly (newest version);
  `#scene=SRC-54.2` pins V2. `#set=TEMPLE` opens your whole running order.

## Three rules of the road

1. **Work on your own scenes** (SRC-51 to 55, and the TEMPLE set list — the recycled four belong to their keepers). Ask
   in the group chat before touching someone else's.
2. **Say in the chat when you're publishing** ("working on Ascension tonight")
   so two people aren't pushing at the same moment.
3. **If something looks wrong, nothing is lost.** Tell Claude: *"the last
   change broke it — go back to how it was."*

## Sound

Each scene makes its own sound in the browser AND sends MIDI to Ableton (the
real show sound). Your first Ableton session with Lance is a "rig walk":
one instrument at a time, agreeing what plays on each channel. Until then,
tune the browser sound — it is the sketch the rig will be finished from.
