THE CAVE · SOURCE

Map your controller

The source speaks two numbers: a left hand and a right hand. Bind any MIDI controller — a fader, a knob, a real theremin — to each.

Coaching: hit CONNECT and allow MIDI. Press LEARN L, then sweep your left control through its whole range for ~2 seconds — the page hears which control moved and binds it, whether it speaks CC, pitch bend, or pressure. Repeat for R. Bindings persist across reloads. No hardware? The on-screen theremin, scene surface, edge lasers, and W/S · ↑/↓ keys all drive the hands.

Calibrate the sensors


    
The LEARN sweep already stored each control's working range, so full reach reaches 1.00. Two things are worth doing on the show machine: press SET REST with nobody at the instrument — a rangefinder streams a value whether or not a hand is there, and this is what lets scenes go idle and tease again — and flip INVERT if the readout drops when you reach outward. Every scene expects at the source = 0, arm's reach = 1.

    HOW THIS WALL WORKS
    
    
    The source is an instrument with exactly two continuous inputs — a left hand and a right hand — each broadcast as a MIDI CC (0–127). Every piece in this library is a study in what light and sound can do with only those two numbers.
    
    
    Ambient mode
    
    
    When nobody is touching the controls, two slow "ghost hands" drift on their own LFOs. The whole wall breathes with them — that's the resting state of the installation.
    
    
    Taking control
    
    
      
      
    • Click a piece to focus it fullscreen.
      • 
      
    • Pointer: press and move on the left half of the screen to drive L, right half to drive R. Like the real instrument: hands at the source rest at zero — reach outward for more.
      • 
      
    • Touch: two fingers at once = two hands.
      • 
      
    • Laser sliders: drag the beams at either edge.
      • 
      
    • Keyboard: hold W/S for L, ↑/↓ for R.
      • 
      
    • Hardware: open MAP in the left rail — connect, then LEARN each hand by sweeping your control through its full range. Two different controllers can drive the two hands.
      • 
    
    
    
    Let go for six seconds and the ghost hands take that channel back.

R reseeds a scene while it is open, ←/→ walk the wall, P toggles the projector frame. Steal any of these paradigms for your own source piece.
    
    
    
  
    

    



    
  
    
    
    THE RIG — ROLE → CHANNEL ROUTING
    
    
    The rig is global: one mapping, identical for every scene. A scene never remaps channels — it just plays a subset of the roles below, so your Ableton set works for the whole wall.
    
    
    Every musical role the pieces generate is routed to a MIDI channel. Give each of your Ableton instruments its own channel (track's MIDI From = IAC, Ch. N) and one browser drives the whole rack. Remap freely — changes apply instantly.
    
    
    
    
    Expression
    
    
    CC1 / CC2 = left / right hand, streamed always — enable Remote on the IAC input in Live's MIDI prefs, then Cmd+M-map any knob (filter, macro, send) to a hand. CC74 is sent per role channel as that layer's energy — map it to each instrument's filter cutoff for free movement.
